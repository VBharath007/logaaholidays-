1. BRAND & DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Honeymoon Packages landing page embodies a seamless fusion of:
  • Ultra-luxury romantic escapism (gentle curves, glassmorphism, ethereal transitions).
  • Logaa Holidays signature visual identity:
      - Deep forest green: #0B5D3E (--color-primary-forest)
      - Rich emerald green: #167A58 (--color-primary-emerald)
      - Ocean blue accent: #0284C7 (--color-blue-ocean)
      - Soft gold highlight: #D4A017 (--color-accent-gold)
      - Luxury background: #F5F7F9 (--color-bg-luxury)
      - Primary typography: Cormorant Garamond serif (font-display)
      - Secondary typography: Outfit sans-serif (font-body)
      - Delicate script accents: Great Vibes / Italian script
  • Elevated travel aesthetic:
      - Smooth hover scale transforms
      - Rich dark vignette gradients behind high-contrast text
      - Pill badges with soft translucent backdrop blur (backdrop-blur-md)
      - Floating trust cards and micro-interactions
      - No generic flat Bootstrap containers; only tailored, organic, and sculptural cards.
